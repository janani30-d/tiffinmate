HOME CHEF GUIDELINES

Simple Guidelines for Trusted Homemade Food

TiffinMate keeps the process simple while helping home chefs maintain a reliable, safe, and trusted experience for every customer.

01

Who Can Join?

Home cooks who enjoy preparing homemade food and want to share their cooking with customers can apply to become a TiffinMate home chef.

  • Passion for homemade cooking
  • Willingness to serve local customers
  • Ability to prepare food consistently
02

Kitchen & Food Standards

Food should be prepared in a clean and well-maintained home kitchen with attention to freshness, hygiene, and proper handling.

  • Clean food preparation area
  • Fresh and quality ingredients
  • Safe food preparation practices
03

Menu & Pricing Guidelines

Keep your menu clear and accurate so customers can easily understand what you offer before placing an order.

  • Clearly describe every dish
  • Keep prices accurate and updated
  • Mention important meal details
04

Orders & Delivery Expectations

Prepare confirmed orders on time and make sure meals are packed appropriately for a smooth customer experience.

  • Follow your listed preparation times
  • Pack meals securely
  • Keep customers informed when needed
05

Customer Service

A friendly and reliable experience helps build long-term relationships with customers who choose your homemade food.

  • Communicate clearly with customers
  • Respond to order-related questions
  • Treat every customer respectfully
06

Verification Requirements

Provide accurate information during the application process so TiffinMate can review your home chef profile.

  • Provide accurate profile information
  • Complete the required verification steps
  • Keep your chef information updated
Simple guidelines. Trusted homemade food. These guidelines help create a consistent, transparent, and reliable experience for both home chefs and customers.
